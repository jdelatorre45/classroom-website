YOU DON’T HAVE TO FIGURE IT OUT ALONE

Progress starts
with one small step.

Feeling stuck? Use these starting points to get moving again. Asking for help is part of learning.

I’m not sure how to start my analysis.
Choose one short quotation. Name a precise feature, explain what it suggests in this context, and connect its effect to the writer’s purpose or audience.
I need help planning my writing.
Identify your audience, purpose, and text type. List three key ideas, put them in a useful order, and draft an opening that fits your reader.
How can I use feedback?
Pick one improvement you can act on. Revise a sentence or paragraph, compare both versions, and explain why your change makes the writing stronger.
Read with curiosity. ✦ Write with purpose. ✦ Grow with confidence.
