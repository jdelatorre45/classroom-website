THE HEART BEHIND THE CLASSROOM

Hi, I’m Ms. Palarca.
I’m so glad you’re here.

I teach English at Coconut Creek High School, where I help students explore how language works and discover what they have to say.

My classroom brings together thoughtful reading, purposeful writing, and plenty of creativity. From analyzing a writer’s choices to finding the courage to share an idea, every step is a chance to grow.

Here, we celebrate effort, ask good questions, and make room for every voice.

With purpose & a little pink,
